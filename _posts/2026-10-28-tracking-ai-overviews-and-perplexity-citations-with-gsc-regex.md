# Tracking AI Overviews and Perplexity Citations Using Google Search Console Regex Filters

*By **[Sergi Garcia](https://pulspolski24.pl/autor-piotr-zielinski/)** ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sergi-garcia-68573a447/)) - Technical SEO Specialist at [Puls Polski 24](https://pulspolski24.pl/)*

Generative AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity) prioritize pages that combine three elements:

1. **A concise 45 to 55 word factual summary** immediately after the main heading (`.aeo-direct-answer`),
2. **A clean HTML `<table>`** presenting structured numbers, dates, or technical specs,
3. **Outbound links to authoritative primary sources** (`.gov.pl`, `.edu.pl`, [IPN](https://ipn.gov.pl/), [URE](https://www.ure.gov.pl/)) paired with verified author entities (`ProfilePage`).
